Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 30.10.2026

MuscleUp (“the App”) respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and how we protect it.

1. Information We Collect

When you create an account in MuscleUp, we collect the following personal information:

  • First name
  • Last name
  • Email address
  • Username
  • Gender
  • Year of birth
  • Body weight

Your password is handled by Firebase Authentication and is never stored by the App. If you sign in with Google, we receive your name and email address from Google.

While you use the App we also store your workout data: training plans, completed sessions, statistics, personal records, badges, streaks, XP, weight history, friends and coach training plans.

Progress photos and music files you add stay on your device and are never uploaded.

2. How We Use Your Information

The personal information collected is used solely for:

  • Account creation and login authentication
  • Displaying your username and results in the leaderboard
  • Managing your user profile within the app
  • Synchronizing your data between your devices

We do not use your personal information for marketing purposes. We do not sell or rent your personal data.

3. Information Visible to Other Users

Other signed-in users can see your username, league, XP, gender and year of birth in the leaderboard, and can open your public profile (name, statistics, records and badges) unless you make your profile private in Settings. When you send a training plan as a coach, the recipient receives the plan together with your exercise lists and custom sessions.

4. Camera and AI Data

MuscleUp uses camera and AI features to support exercise tracking and form analysis.

No video recordings, images, or AI-processed data are stored, saved, or distributed. All camera and AI processing occurs locally on your device.

5. Data Storage and Service Providers

Your data is stored using Google Firebase services (Authentication and Cloud Firestore). The App also uses Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics, which collect app usage events, device information and crash reports to help us improve stability. Google processes this data on our behalf. We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data from unauthorized access, loss, or misuse.

6. Data Sharing

Apart from the service providers above, we do not share your personal data with third parties, except as required by law.

7. Children

The App is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

8. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Update or correct your information
  • Delete your account and associated data

You can delete your account at any time in the App: Settings → Delete account (you may be asked to log in again first), or on the web at cpucademy.github.io/muscleup/delete-account.html. This immediately and permanently deletes your sign-in account, your profile and workout data, your leaderboard entry and league, your coach status and the training plans coaches sent you. Deleting in the App also removes the App's data and progress photos from that device. Training plans you sent to other users as a coach stay with them. Deletion cannot be undone. For other requests, such as access to or correction of your data, contact us.

9. Contact Information

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or your personal data, you may contact us at: cpucademy@gmail.com

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected within the app and on this page. Continued use of the app after changes indicates your acceptance of the updated policy.

By using MuscleUp, you agree to this Privacy Policy.
